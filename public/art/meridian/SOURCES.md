# Meridian surface sources

The game ships local WebP derivatives of these Poly Haven CC0 assets. Color maps use a neutral grayscale range so the Verdant Studio finish can supply the tint. Normal maps retain their OpenGL channel orientation. Surface images fit within 768 × 768 pixels, and the sky panorama fits within 1536 × 768 pixels.

| --- | --- | --- |
| masonry | [Sandstone Blocks 05](https://polyhaven.com/a/sandstone_blocks_05) | Diffuse and nor_gl, 1k |
| stucco | [Plastered Wall 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_02) | Diffuse and nor_gl, 1k |
| tarmac | [Asphalt 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_04) | Diffuse and nor_gl, 1k |
| cladding | [Corrugated Iron 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/corrugated_iron_02) | Diffuse and nor_gl, 1k |
| timber | [Oak Wood Planks](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_wood_planks) | Diffuse and nor_gl, 1k |
| cobbles | [Cobblestone Floor 08](https://polyhaven.com/a/cobblestone_floor_08) | Diffuse and nor_gl, 1k, WebP quality 83 |
| limewash | [Red Plaster Weathered](https://polyhaven.com/a/red_plaster_weathered) | Diffuse and nor_gl, 1k sources downsampled to 768, WebP quality 80 |
