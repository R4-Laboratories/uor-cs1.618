OPERATOR REFERENCE / 01

Every round has a purpose.

cs1.618 is an original tactical browser FPS. Meridian has two relay sites, A and B. Each round begins with a buy phase and ends with one team winning. First to eight rounds wins the match.

EMBER / ATTACKERS

The device carrier is marked on your radar. Bring it to either site. Stand still and hold E for three seconds to plant. Protect it until the 40-second timer expires. A teammate can pick up a dropped device by walking over it.

TIDAL / DEFENDERS

Keep both sites secure. If a device is planted, stand beside it and hold E for ten seconds to defuse, or five seconds with a kit. You must still defuse after eliminating all attackers.

Precision pays.

Standing still makes shots more accurate. Moving and jumping widen your spread. Sustained fire adds recoil; fire short bursts and compensate with your mouse. Game damage depends on the weapon, range and hit zone. A vest and helmet absorb part of a torso or head hit while armor points remain. Leg hits bypass armor and have a lower damage multiplier. Reloading takes time and moving away cancels an interaction. There is no friendly damage, but teammates block bullets.

Find your route

Customs Arcade links the western landings to A. Pump Hall opens onto the eastern approaches to B. The two districts connect back into West Walk, Mid Court and East Arcade. Your radar shows the site letters and the locations of teammates.

Buy for the next round.

You begin with $800 and a P9. Buy during the freeze phase or the first 25 seconds of the live round while near your spawn. Survivors keep their equipment. Eliminated players return with a pistol. Rivet replaces the starting sidearm. Tern is a semi-automatic carbine. Breaker spends one shell per six-pellet shot. Both teams earn round rewards, and kills and successful planting earn extra money.

Play together — directly in your browsers.

Choose Create browser room, copy its invitation, and send it to friends. They open the link and choose Join invitation. The creator's browser runs the match; WebRTC carries the game between players. Empty slots can be filled with bots. A player joining after a round starts spectates until the next round. Keep the host tab open and active: closing it ends the room. Automatic host migration is not implemented.

Signaling helps peers find each other and STUN helps establish direct connections. Some routers and mobile networks require a TURN relay; enter your provider's URL, username and credential in Connection settings on both ends. GitHub Pages cannot host STUN or TURN. You can also configure an HTTPS PeerServer for signaling; all players must use the same service.

For a persistent match, choose Dedicated game server and share the same server address and room code. Practice with bots runs entirely in your browser. Your career

Open Prism career to choose a profile and see lifetime kills, deaths and K/D. Practice and multiplayer have separate records. Export a signed backup to carry those totals to another linked device. Keep access to the original identity for recovery.

Built with Verdant.

The game uses Verdant Engine's WebAssembly spatial kernel for collision, hit queries and audio, with WebGL2 presentation. Its round model is generated through Prism-DevRel and checked with LexLean and PrismPM; UORust maps the Rust bridge into Prism facets. The repository documents the exact verification scope.

Weapon feel & performance

Stop moving for tighter shots. Crouching improves accuracy. Sustained automatic fire follows an original repeatable spray pattern; short pauses reset the burst. Use the pause menu to choose automatic, native or performance rendering. Quality, volume and mouse sensitivity are saved in this browser.
